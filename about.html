About LinkdIn

Connecting professionals worldwide through innovative networking solutions. Our mission is to empower individuals and organizations to build meaningful professional relationships and unlock opportunities.

Our Story

Founded in 2023, LinkdIn emerged from a simple yet powerful idea: that professional networking should be accessible, meaningful, and impactful for everyone. We set out to create a platform that goes beyond traditional networking by fostering genuine connections and facilitating career growth.

Today, LinkdIn serves millions of professionals across the globe, helping them discover opportunities, share insights, and build the careers they deserve. Our platform combines cutting-edge technology with human-centered design to create an experience that empowers professionals at every stage of their journey.

50M+ Registered Users

2M+ Jobs Posted

Our Mission

To create economic opportunity for every member of the global workforce by connecting professionals with opportunities, insights, and each other. We believe that when people are empowered to build meaningful connections, they can achieve extraordinary things.

Our Vision

To be the world's most trusted professional networking platform, where every connection leads to opportunity and every professional can realize their full potential.

Our Values

  • 🤝 Integrity: We build trust through honest, transparent, and ethical behavior in everything we do.
  • 🚀 Innovation: We constantly innovate to create better experiences and opportunities for our users.
  • 🌟 Excellence: We strive for excellence in every aspect of our platform and user experience.
  • 🌍 Inclusivity: We believe in creating opportunities for professionals from all backgrounds and industries.
  • 💼 Professionalism: We maintain the highest standards of professionalism in our platform and community.
